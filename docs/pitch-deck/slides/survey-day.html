The three farms run through quality checks, analysis, hotspot extraction, a PDF report and an SMS to the farmer, in about ten seconds. What is real and what is simulated, say it plainly. The imagery is real: a rice research station from a public UAV dataset (CC BY 4.0), stitched from its patches. Farm 1 is an untouched real block and the pipeline correctly finds nothing. Farms 2 and 3 have stress patches blended into the real bands at known locations. They are not real pest damage. We did this because the real blighted plots in that dataset looked the same as the grass around them in our analysis, so there was no real anomaly this pipeline could find. The result is mixed and we show it as it is: 3 of 7 planted patches found, all of them strong ones; the mild and small patches were missed. Nearly every pixel we flagged was inside a planted patch, so false alarms were rare. Hotspots show LOW confidence because the dataset does not state its radiometric calibration.
