This is how we answer challenge 3, the labelled-data problem, and it is also the honest route to any accuracy claim. A scout walks to a ranked hotspot and taps one of three answers: pest damage seen, no pest damage, or other / not sure. The verdict is stored against that hotspot, the survey, who recorded it, and the model version. We do not retrain on verdicts automatically. Collect them first, review label quality, then use them to calibrate thresholds and to train and validate anything learned. Until that data exists we make no accuracy claim, which is the point of the evidence ladder two slides on.
