10 / 14

Four tests, from four different data sources, so no single dataset carries the story. Planted stress on real rice: our own simulator-style test. The two anomaly farms contain 7 patches in total; 3 were found, and the ones found had severity of 0.7 or more. The patches missed were milder, whatever their size. That is seven patches, so treat it as an observation, not a rate. Maize: two real top-down mosaics from a public dataset. The labels were derived from vegetation-index thresholds, which flatters the index and says little about the visual model. Rice blight: top-down paddy with expert subplot labels. Low and high severity were spectrally almost identical (mean NDVI 0.205 against 0.193), so every score is weak here. We also found healthy and blighted rice sit in different fields in that dataset, so a healthy-versus-blight score is confounded by site; we used the within-site severity comparison instead. Cashew: the only set with pest, disease and abiotic labels, but the images are close-range and oblique, so it says nothing certain about aerial surveys.
