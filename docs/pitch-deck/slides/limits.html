What AgriScout does not do

It does not name the pest.

Hotspots are places to inspect. The cause and the species need someone on the ground.

No pest-labelled aerial test.

We found no public top-down pest data. The damage on the rice farms is simulated.

It misses mild and small stress.

On simulated damage it found the larger patches (4 of 6) and missed small satellite spots. Almost nothing else was flagged.

A uniformly stressed field passes.

Each field is compared with itself, so nothing stands out when everything is affected.

The visual check is weak.

Near chance on real maize and rice. It carries 30% of the score, a weight set after seeing our planted-patch results.

Prototype, not field-proven.

No drone flown, no scouts yet. SMS is a mock and Kannada is unreviewed. Low confidence reflects unknown imagery calibration.

Read this slide slowly. It is the reason the rest of the deck is believable. 1. We do not name the pest. This is the problem statement’s own limit and our product follows it. 2. We looked for public top-down pest-labelled data and did not find usable data. The only labelled pest, disease and abiotic set we found (cashew) is close-range and oblique. The two anomaly farms in the demo use simulated stress on real rice imagery. 3. In that test the pipeline found 4 of 6 simulated patches (at least 30 percent of each flagged) and missed small satellite spots; it flagged 34 percent of the simulated damage at Kikkeri and 76 percent at Maddur. About 99 to 100 percent of the flagged area was inside a simulated patch. High precision, modest recall. A badly damaged patch that opens wide onto a path can also be masked out as bare ground; we closed narrow gaps but wide ones remain a limit. 4. Because each field is scored against itself, a field that is stressed everywhere has no outlier. It would pass. A reference from a healthy neighbour could help, but a reference learned at one rice site did not transfer to another in our tests. 5. The visual texture model was near chance on real maize and rice. We kept it as a minor, clearly labelled input. Its 30% weight was chosen after we saw results on the planted patches, so do not read the simulated-damage result as independent proof. Thresholds are also provisional. 6. No drone was flown and no scout has used it yet. SMS is a mock gateway; a one-time code proves control of a phone, not identity; Kannada text needs review; every hotspot shows low confidence because the dataset does not state its radiometric calibration. The farmers in the demo are fictional. Then say what we do about it: the next slide.
