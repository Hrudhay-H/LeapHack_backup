AgriScout AI · Problem 4

03 / 14

The five challenges are straight from the problem statement. We show our own status for each, including the two we have not solved. Status words are deliberately plain: Open means we have a mechanism but no evidence it works on real pests; Partly met means tested, with limits; Built, not field-tested means the software exists and runs end to end on real imagery with simulated stress. Challenge 2: we tested at 4.4 cm per pixel (stitched from 1.1 cm source patches). Severity mattered more than size in our small test, see the evidence slides. Challenge 3: we looked for public top-down pest-labelled data and did not find usable data. The scout verdict loop is our answer.
