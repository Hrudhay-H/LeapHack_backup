The page on the right is a real generated report for the Kikkeri plot, including the simulated-stress banner across the top. The report is generated once per survey and stored; the farmer dashboard and the operator desk both serve the same file, so there is nothing to keep in sync. Priority tells a scout how urgently to look, not how severe a disease is. Confidence blends image quality, agreement between the two signals and patch size; it is not a probability of damage. The pattern hint (compact, elongated, edge-aligned, broad) is a rule on the shape and position of the hotspot and is worded as “consistent with”, never as a cause. Kannada wording is illustrative and needs review by a Kannada-speaking agricultural expert before real use. Say so if asked.
